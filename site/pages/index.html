Pricing

Brokerage is one line. Your cost is the whole list.

A headline brokerage rate tells you very little on its own. Statutory and regulatory charges apply on every trade in India regardless of which broker you use, and regardless of what brokerage plan you are on. So we show you both, separately.

Trade Grow sets this

Brokerage. The only charge we control and the only one we keep. Our published rate is on the pricing page, with the conditions that apply to it.

Government / Exchange / SEBI sets this

STT, GST, Stamp Duty, Exchange charges, SEBI fees, DP charges. Levied by law. We collect and remit them. No broker in India can waive these, and any broker suggesting otherwise is not being straight with you.

Security

The rule that protects you from us, too

No one from Trade Grow will ever ask for your OTP, password, PIN, UPI PIN or card PIN. Not a sales agent, not a support executive, not someone claiming to be a manager. There is no legitimate situation in which we need those.

If anyone asks you for these in our name, they are committing fraud — and we would like to know about it.
