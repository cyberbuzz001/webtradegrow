What this platform does not do

Worth stating plainly, because some platforms blur this line:

  • It does not give buy or sell recommendations, tips or targets
  • It does not promise or project returns
  • It does not offer any assured or guaranteed outcome on any product
  • It does not trade on your behalf or manage your portfolio for you

Trade Grow is execution and reporting infrastructure. Every decision on it is yours, and every market risk on it is yours.
