Account opening

What happens, step by step

Before you start, it is worth knowing exactly what you will be asked for and why. Nothing below should be a surprise once you begin, and nothing below involves sharing a credential with a person.

{{comp:otpWarning}}
Read this before you start. A Trade Grow representative may help you understand the process, answer questions, or stay on a call while you complete it yourself. What they will never do is ask you to read out an OTP, tell them your password, install a screen-sharing app, or complete the form on your behalf using your credentials. If that happens, stop, and report it through Support.

  1. Mobile number

    Enter the mobile number you want registered on the account. Use a number that is linked to your Aadhaar, since later verification steps depend on it. This number will receive all trade and account alerts, so it should be one you personally control.

  2. OTP verification

    You will receive a one-time password. Enter it on the Trade Grow screen yourself. Nobody from Trade Grow needs it, will ask for it, or has any use for it.

  3. PAN

    Your PAN is mandatory for a trading and demat account under SEBI rules. It is validated against income tax records, and the name on your PAN must match the name on your bank account. A mismatch here is the single most common cause of rejected applications.

  4. KYC information

    Your address, date of birth, occupation, income bracket and other details required under KYC rules. If you are already KYC-verified with a SEBI-registered KYC Registration Agency, parts of this may be fetched for you, which shortens the process.

  5. Bank details

    The bank account that will be linked for funding and payouts. It must be in your own name — payouts can legally go only to your own account. Third-party transfers, including from a family member’s account, are not permitted.

  6. Required verification

    Depending on the segments you want, this can include in-person verification (typically completed digitally via a short video), a signature specimen, and income proof if you are activating derivatives. You will be told which apply to you before you begin.

  7. e-Sign

    You sign the account opening form electronically using the Aadhaar-based e-sign service. This generates an OTP on your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Once again: enter it yourself, on the official screen.

  8. Application submission

    Your completed application goes for internal verification and then for the regulatory steps required to open a trading and demat account. You will receive an application reference number — keep it, as support will ask for it.

  9. Account status and activation

    You can check your application status with your reference number at any point. On activation you receive your client code and demat details. Your welcome communication comes from our official channels only, never from an individual’s personal number.

What to keep ready

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar, with your current mobile number linked to it
  • A bank account in your own name, with account number and IFSC
  • A signature on plain white paper, photographed clearly
  • Income proof, only if you intend to activate F&O
  • Nominee details, if you wish to add one at this stage

The precise list applicable to your application is shown inside the flow. Where this page and the application differ, the application is correct.

Before you complete KYC, consider doing this first

We would rather you opened an account having checked us than opened one quickly. If you have not yet:

  • Verify our registration details against SEBI and the exchanges
  • Read the full charge list, including charges outside the trade
  • Compare us with what you use today
  • Read the fraud red flags, which apply to every platform

None of that costs you anything, and an account opened after those checks is worth more to us than one opened before them.

Common reasons applications get delayed

Most rejections are avoidable and none of them are mysterious.

  • !
    Name mismatch between PAN and bank account

    Including initials, expanded surnames, or a maiden name on one and a married name on the other.

  • !
    Mobile number not linked to Aadhaar

    The e-sign step sends an OTP to the Aadhaar-linked number. If that is an old number you no longer use, the step cannot complete.

  • !
    Unclear document images

    Glare, crops that cut off an edge, or photographs of a screen rather than the document.

  • !
    Signature mismatch

    The specimen signature should match the one on your bank records, not a rushed version of it.

  • !
    Missing income proof for derivatives

    F&O activation requires income proof. Without it, the account can still open for equity, with derivatives added later.
